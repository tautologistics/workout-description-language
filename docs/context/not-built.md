# What is not built

The real holes. Not the same as [deliberately-not-done.md](deliberately-not-done.md),
which is the list of things that will *stay* unbuilt and why.

- ~~**Results.**~~ **Built 2026-08-23** (queue item 2). A `.wdlr` record, the board
  that writes one, and `measure: load` over a series of them. See
  [what-exists.md](what-exists.md). What is *still* missing from it is narrower
  than the hole was: **structured modifications** — a movement swapped on the day
  goes in `notes:`, because whether they want structure is an open question
  (roadmap §3.2) and reading one would have settled it by accident.
- **A history view.** Results exist and nothing draws them. `wdl result --goals`
  answers "did the load go up"; nothing answers "show me this workout over the
  last three months", and neither app knows a `.wdlr` exists once it has been
  downloaded.
- **Sharing, accounts, a platform.** Deliberately far off.
- **Whiteboard photo → WDL.** Interesting, separate project.

The piece of this that is ready to start, with what it touches, is in
[ready-to-pick-up.md](ready-to-pick-up.md).
